ROUTE & OPERATION

Niger Inland Waterway

The vessel was developed specifically for passenger transportation along the Niger Inland Waterway, connecting Lokoja with Onitsha.

The route requires a vessel capable of providing reliable passenger transport while maintaining suitable speed, maneuverability, comfort and safety.
